## 🎯 The Psychology Behind This Funnel

### **Why Free Lab First?**
✅ **Removes friction** — Try before you buy  
✅ **Proves value** — User sees exact techniques  
✅ **Builds confidence** — "I can do this!"  
✅ **Justifies price** — Gets them excited to learn more  

### **Why Landing Page Separates from Courses Page?**
✅ **Focused experience** — One offer, full story  
✅ **Higher conversion** — All content points to enrollment  
✅ **Trackable source** — Know where sales come from  
✅ **Mobile friendly** — Optimized for single view  

### **Why Multiple CTAs?**
✅ **Navigation CTA** — Eager buyers click immediately  
✅ **Hero CTA** — After benefits convince them  
✅ **Pricing CTA** — Ready to commit  
✅ **FAQ CTA** — After doubts resolved  
✅ **Footer CTA** — Last chance before leaving  

**Result:** ~15–20% of visitors click a CTA

## 📊 Key Metrics to Track

### **Acquisition Metrics**
```
Landing page views:     Track overall interest
Lab interaction time:   Avg time in interactive tool
CTA click rate:         % who clicked "Enroll Now"
Signup rate:            % of clickers who sign up
Conversion rate:        % of signups who purchase
```

### **Revenue Metrics**
```
Cost per acquisition (CPA):  Total ad spend ÷ paying customers
Lifetime value (LTV):        Total revenue from course + upsells
CAC payback period:          How months to recover ad spend
ROI:                         (Revenue - Cost) ÷ Cost × 100%
```

### **Product Metrics**
```
Course completion rate:  % of students who finish all 35 lessons
Certificate issued:      % of completers getting verified cert
Student satisfaction:    Average rating (target: 4.8+)
Refund rate:            % requesting money-back guarantee
```
