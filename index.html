Manufacturing Flow

From RFQ to
Production

Our concurrent engineering approach compresses lead times without sacrificing quality or traceability.

Phase 01
Inquiry & Feasibility

RFQ received → feasibility study → commercial discussion → PO → development plan. NDA signed before any design discussion.

Phase 02
Concept & Design

Concept 3D print for form validation → detailed CAD design → manufacturing drawing review → internal approval. Feedback loop until client sign-off.

Phase 03
Manufacturing & Trial

Tool room / machining process → assembly → trial run → First Part Inspection (FPI) → rework if needed → Sample Submission.

Phase 04
Approval & Production

First Part Approval (FPA) → tool validation → Pilot LOT → full production run → dispatched with complete documentation.

Manufacturing
Facilities

State-of-the-art machinery under one roof — VMC, EDM, Wire cut, Laser, Press Brake, Injection Moulding.

CNC / VMC Centre
Jyoti RX-20 and Chakra BMV-60 vertical machining centres. Multi-axis milling, turning, and boring for mould cavities and precision components.
Wire Cut & EDM
Job Master wire cut and sinker EDM machines. Micron-level tolerances on hardened tool steels, punch-die sets, electrode work, and complex profiles.
Fiber Laser Cutting
Perfect Laser high-power fiber laser cutter for sheet metal. Clean, burr-free edges on MS, SS, and aluminium up to 12mm thickness.
Press Brake (Bending)
ADIRA CNC press brake for precision sheet metal bending. Programmable back-gauge for repeat accuracy on complex bend sequences.
3D Printing Lab
FDM, SLA, and SLS machines. Materials: ABS, PP, PC, Nylon PA11/PA12 (30% glass filled), Rubber, Transparent. Concept models to functional prototypes.
Injection Moulding
ONGO horizontal and vertical injection moulding machines. ABS, PP, Nylon, PC and rubber-grade materials. Short runs to high-volume production.
