Frequently asked questions

Do I need a doctor's referral to visit?
No referral is required. You can walk in or call us directly to book a consultation with our physiotherapy team.

It depends on your condition. After the first assessment, we'll explain a realistic treatment timeline for your recovery.

Yes, we support both acute injury recovery and ongoing management of chronic conditions with personalised plans.
