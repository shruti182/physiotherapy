Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information across the Learners Academy platform.

Last updated: August 13, 2026

At Learners Academy, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our learning platform — whether you're a school student, a working professional, or an instructor.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you register an account, enroll in a course, complete lessons, book a live class, or contact us. This includes:

  • Account data: your name, email address, password, and platform role (Student, Teacher, or Admin).
  • Usage & progress data: completed lessons, course enrollment status, quiz results, notes, and session activity.
  • Interactive activity: questions posted in doubt sessions, live class bookings, and messages sent through the platform.
  • Payment information: processed securely through our payment partners for paid courses — we do not store your full card details on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Personalize your learning dashboard and track your progress across courses.
  • Send transaction receipts, reminders, and notifications about upcoming live classes.
  • Enable live classroom sessions, with teachers joining as broadcasters and students as viewers.
  • Issue certificates upon course completion and verify their authenticity if requested.
  • Improve our courses and platform based on how they're used.

3. Data Security & Storage

We use industry-standard administrative and technical safeguards to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. Some session and progress information is stored locally in your browser to keep the platform fast and responsive — clearing your browser cache or logging out may reset locally saved progress markers, though your enrollment and completed-course records remain safely stored on our servers.

All data transmitted between your device and our platform is encrypted in transit.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited information with trusted service providers who help us run the platform (such as payment processing and email delivery), and only to the extent needed for them to provide that service.

5. Your Rights & Choices

You can review and update your account details at any time from your dashboard. To request a copy of your data, ask us to correct inaccurate information, or request deletion of your account and associated data, write to us at info@learners-academy.co.in.

6. Children's Privacy

Learners Academy offers courses for school students from Grade 6 onward. Where a student is a minor, we recommend a parent or guardian review this policy and supervise account creation and use of the platform.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how your data is handled, reach out to us at info@learners-academy.co.in or +91 7409859114.
